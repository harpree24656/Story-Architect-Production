Our Story

Born From a Writer's Frustration

Story Architect started in 2024 when our founder couldn't find a single tool that let them manage characters, events, relationships, and world lore in one place.

Spreadsheets were messy. Note apps lacked structure. So we decided to build the tool we always wished existed.

Today, Story Architect helps thousands of writers build richer, more structured stories. And we're just getting started.
