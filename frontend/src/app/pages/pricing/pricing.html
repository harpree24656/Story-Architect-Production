Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing.

Can I switch plans later? +

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial for Pro? +

Absolutely! Every new user gets a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan. No credit card required.

What happens to my data if I downgrade? +

Your data is never deleted. Pro features become read-only. You can still access and export your content.

Do you offer refunds? +

We offer a full refund within the first 7 days of any paid subscription.

Can I use it for commercial projects? +

Yes! All plans allow commercial use. Your stories belong entirely to you.
