Into the Wild

The gates of Blackwood Keep were nothing but ash and memory now. Arin stood at the edge of the Ashwood Forest, the last place he'd ever thought he'd find himself — alone, wounded, and stripped of everything he once held dear.

The forest stretched endlessly before him, a dark canopy of ancient oaks and twisted pines. The air was thick with moisture and the scent of decaying leaves.

"Keep moving," he muttered to himself, gripping the hilt of his broken sword.

He walked for hours. The sun never quite reached the forest floor, and the shadows played tricks on his eyes.

Then he heard it — a voice. Soft, melodic, and completely out of place in this forsaken woodland.

"You're bleeding," the voice said.

Arin spun around, raising his broken blade. A woman stood behind a cluster of ferns, her dark hair braided with wildflowers.

"Who are you?" Arin demanded.

"Someone who can help," she said simply. "If you let me."
